Repeated entries (confused, disappointed, enter, search engine, key word, rating/review, filter) share a record. Separate noun/verb meanings such as travel and to travel remain distinct. Combined textbook actions download/upload and turn off/on are separate practice items. Abbreviations sb/sth are expanded to someone/something. The optional capital (city) is taught as capital city, with capital shown as an alternative. Devices is normalised to device. Titles, exercise numbers, example-only words, pronunciation guides, and category headings are not separate vocabulary targets.

There are **188 stable entries**. English examples and visual scene descriptions were newly written for this course. The textbook's two as entries are presented as two senses; the earlier source row's German gloss and example do not agree exactly, so the app supplies a clear new example instead of copying the inconsistent example.

Audio uses packaged synthetic English speech, generated locally from the exact spoken word/expression and example sentence in the content bundle. It is not a collection of human voice recordings. Every item has both normal playback and speed-adjusted playback.

Illustrations are arranged as twelve 4×4 atlases, with each item referencing its own cell. The final four cells of the last board are decorative and do not represent vocabulary targets. Generation prompts are retained in illustration-prompts.json. Exact image files are retained in the source tree for repeatable builds.
